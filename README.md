# ORION — Interactive Product Launch Experience

ORION is an independent product-launch prototype exploring how a guided visual experience can help shoppers discover and understand a product.

## Problem

Product pages can present many specifications at once, making it difficult for shoppers to explore product features in a clear and engaging sequence.

## Product Concept

ORION organizes product discovery into a guided journey:

**Teaser → Reveal → Features → Camera → Chip → Colors → Pricing → Done**

Side navigation allows users to explore different sections and move through the experience at their own pace.

## Key Features

- **Product Reveal:** Introduces the product through a staged visual experience.
- **Feature Exploration:** Organizes product information into dedicated sections.
- **Camera & Chip:** Presents selected technical features.
- **Color Selection:** Allows users to explore product color options.
- **Pricing:** Provides a dedicated view for pricing information.
- **Side Navigation:** Helps users navigate between sections.

## My Approach

I built this prototype out of curiosity, inspired by the presentation style of major consumer-product launches. I wanted to explore whether I could create a similar guided experience and organize product information into a structured user journey.

## Project Status

**Independent interactive concept prototype.**

The prototype explores a product-discovery experience. It has not undergone structured user testing, and no improvement in purchase conversion or user engagement has been established.
